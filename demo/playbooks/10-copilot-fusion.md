## Steps to show

1. Show one slide with the three execution patterns: Single, Cascade, Critique
   (diagram available at the changelog link below).

3. Show the three effort levels as a simple list: Fusion Standard, Fusion High, Fusion Max.

## Slide content

```text
Single    — one model solves the task directly.
Cascade   — a fast model drafts; a quality check escalates to a stronger model if needed.
Critique  — one model drafts, another reviews, the first revises.

Effort levels: Fusion Standard | Fusion High | Fusion Max
```
